# Muse Avatars — Choose your core

Free avatars for Muse: 119 original companions, each one holding the glowing core that makes them *them*.

Some people have trouble coming up with an idea for an avatar. This pack is for them:
browse the companions, find the one that feels like you, and take it.

**Choose your core.**

## How to use yours

1. Open the site and browse the pack — filter by category, subcategory, or form (Stuffed / Alive).
2. Click a companion to see it up close: spin it all the way around, and read its type,
   species, personality, signature trait, and core.
3. Hit **Download portrait** in the detail view and set it as your avatar anywhere you like.

Every portrait is **free to download and use as an avatar**. No account, no sign-up.

## What's in the pack

- **119 companions** across Animals (Birds, Felines, Canines, Vulpines, Forest Animals,
  Big Animals, Water Animals, Minibeasts), Plants & Fungi, Robots & Machines,
  Dinos & Dragons, Fantasy & Cosmic, and Dolls & People.
- Series 1–4 plus The Dolls.
- Each companion comes with a full turnaround sheet (front, right, back, left) so you can
  see your pick from every angle before you choose.

## Running the site

It's a plain static site — no build step, no dependencies:

- Open `index.html` in a browser, or
- Host the whole folder on any static host (GitHub Pages works as-is).

## Layout

- `index.html` — the picker (self-contained CSS/JS)
- `companions.js` — the pack data for all 119 companions
- `assets/portraits/` — one downloadable portrait per companion
- `assets/sheets/` — one turnaround sheet per companion
